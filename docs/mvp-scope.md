# Scope and commercial hypothesis

**ICP:** US owner-operated residential cleaners with ~1–10 cleaning teams and a generic “request a quote”/phone-first process, no existing instant estimate tool.

**Hypothesis:** Owners may pay $39/month if the estimator captures sufficiently complete inquiries to reduce repetitive intake questions and follow-up effort. This has NOT been validated by actual buyers.

**Primary objection:** The homeowner's actual condition, square footage and service scope affect pricing. We must never treat the preliminary estimate as a guaranteed quote. Some cleaners may already get a similar flow from their booking software; these are poorer-fit prospects.

**Shareable link:** Allows 60-second experience without a widget integration or changes to the prospect's existing website. Customers can link to /q/slug. This is a product hypothesis, not a claim proven by research.

**End-to-end:** Visitor follows quote link → chooses ZIP/service/bedrooms/bathrooms/frequency/extras → receives server-calculated range → explicitly sends details → lead stored → owner email attempted. Demo uses *fictional* values and never captures real personal data.

**Out of scope:** CRM, SMS, booking calendar, customer dashboard, AI pricing, employee management, site widget, other cleaning verticals, automatic sign-up, auto-generated custom price discovery.

**Deal requirements:** Actual owner price configuration, approved payment provider, adequate notification deliverability, lawyer-reviewed legal policy, contact channel, deployed separate DB.
