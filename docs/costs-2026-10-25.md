# Public provider pricing — reviewed 2026-10-25

- Vercel Hobby $0 is **not licensed for commercial use**. Vercel Pro starts at $20/month (included usage credits; excess usage and taxes may apply): https://vercel.com/pricing
- Supabase Free: $0, generally up to 2 active free projects, but can pause after 1 week inactivity; Pro starts at $25/month: https://supabase.com/pricing
- Resend Free: $0, currently 3,000 transactional emails/month and 100/day: https://resend.com/pricing
- Lemon Squeezy recurring checkout: platform base 5% + $0.50 per transaction, **additional +0.5% subscription processing surcharge**, subject to payment region/method and other edge fees: https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees

For a $39 monthly subscription: $39*5.5%+$0.50 = $2.645 transaction cost (~$2.65), excluding additional charges and FX/taxes. Seven clients = $273 gross MRR; ~$18.52 fees; $254.48 after those estimated fees. With Vercel Pro ($20) and Supabase Pro ($25) and Resend free, baseline infra $45/month → ~$209.48 left before tax, extra fees, cancellations, support and acquisition labor.

For testing only, Supabase Free may reduce baseline infrastructure to $20/month; note the inactivity suspension caveat and evaluate reliability before offering a paid service.
